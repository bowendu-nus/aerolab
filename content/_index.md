## About

The **AERO Lab** studies the air we breathe — the health and cognitive impacts of air pollutants, building ventilation and filtration, and data-driven identification and control of indoor pollution sources.

## Join Us

We are accepting **PhD students for the January 2027 intake (apply by 1 August)**.

To apply, please send your **CV** and a **short cover letter** to **aerolab.nus@gmail.com**.  
Your cover letter should include:

- **Motivations for pursuing a PhD at our lab**  
- **Education background**  
- **Research interests** (2–3 sentences)  
- **Two writing samples** — published journal papers preferred; for each, include a **link** and a **brief summary**  
- **Anticipated starting time**

For further information on admissions, please refer to:  
[NUS CDE Graduate Programme Application Guide](https://cde.nus.edu.sg/graduate/graduate-programmes-by-research/application-period-2/)

NUS offers a range of scholarships for outstanding graduate students. Applicants to AERO Lab’s PhD programme are encouraged to explore:

- [NUS President’s Graduate Fellowship (PGF)](https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/presidents-graduate-fellowship/)  
- [NUS Research Scholarship (RS)](https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/nus-research-scholarship/)
